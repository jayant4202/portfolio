CampaignCrafter: an AI agent that drafts marketing campaigns

Problem
Planning a month of social media content by hand was slow and repetitive: researching dates, writing captions and briefing designs for every post.
Solution
An AI agent that takes a short brief (brand, goals, audience, outcomes, month and colour palette) and builds a campaign calendar with a theme, caption and image for each post.
Impact
▼65% decrease (an improvement) content creation time, turning a manual process into a reviewable first draft.
