Privacy

This site has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no ads and no third-party trackers. It doesn't collect any personal information from visitors.

Your theme choice (dark, light or system) is saved only in your own browser.

Technical logs

To keep the site reliable and secure, each page request produces a short technical log entry: the page address (without any query string), the time, the response status, the approximate country and data centre, and a shortened browser description. IP addresses, cookies and form data are never logged. These entries are viewed live for troubleshooting and are not stored. If your browser reports a blocked script or resource on this site, that report is logged the same way. The hosting provider, Cloudflare, also processes standard request data to protect the site from attacks and bots.
