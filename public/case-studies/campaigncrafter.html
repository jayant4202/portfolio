CampaignCrafter: an AI agent that plans a month of marketing

At Blenheim Chalcot I led discovery and development of an AI agent proof of concept for digital marketing: give it a short brief, and it drafts a full month of social media posts.

RoleAssociate PM (lead)
CompanyBlenheim Chalcot India
TypeAI agent proof of concept
Outcome▼65% decrease (an improvement) creation time

The problem

Planning a month of social media content was a manual, repeated job. For every post, someone had to find the right date or occasion, decide the theme, write the caption and brief a designer. It took a long time, and the work looked much the same every month, which made it a good candidate for automation.

How I approached it

  • Mapped the manual process first. I broke content planning into its steps (understand the brand, set goals, pick dates and themes, write copy, create visuals) to see which steps an AI agent could do and where a person still needed to decide.
  • Designed the brief, not just the model. The quality of the output depends on the input, so the form asks for exactly what a marketer would tell an agency: a company description, goals, target audience, desired outcomes, the number and kind of posts, the month and an optional colour palette.
  • Kept people in control. The agent produces a first draft the team reviews and edits, rather than publishing anything on its own.

The solution

CampaignCrafter is a simple web app. After the brief is submitted, the agent returns a campaign calendar for the month, with posts placed on relevant days. Each post opens to show its theme, a written caption and an AI-generated image. In the demo, a cricket team's brief produces posts such as a festival greeting tied to the team and its fans.

Results

  • ▼65% decrease (an improvement) content creation time, from brief to a reviewable month of posts

What I learned

The biggest lever wasn't the model; it was the brief. Asking for goals, audience and outcomes up front made the drafts far more usable, and keeping a person as the final reviewer made the team comfortable adopting it.
