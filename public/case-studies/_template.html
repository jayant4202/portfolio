AI transaction categorization for a neobank

RoleProduct Manager
TimelineQ1–Q2 2026
Team2 eng, 1 DS, 1 design
Outcome-38% mis-tags

Problem

Users saw ~1 in 6 transactions in the wrong spend category, which broke budgets and drove "why is this marked as shopping?" tickets.

Users & insight

What research you did and the one insight that changed the direction.

Options considered

Expanding the rules engine vs. a pure LLM vs. a hybrid. Trade-offs on cost per transaction, latency, explainability and regulatory comfort.

Key decision: rules handle high-confidence merchants; the LLM only sees the long tail, with confidence thresholds and human review below them.

Execution

How you built evals, ran the pilot, handled PII (masking account numbers before any model call), and rolled out.

Results
