About me

I'm a product manager who likes the hard plumbing of fintech — credit lines, card programs, bank integrations — and the people problems that sit on top of it. I started in AI-led product work at Blenheim Chalcot and Axion, then joined Vegapay's Founder's Office to launch lending and card programs with banks. Today I use AI tools like Claude Code to build the workflows I used to only write PRDs for.
