About me

I'm a product manager who enjoys the hard parts of fintech: credit lines, card programs and bank integrations, and the people problems on top of them. I studied engineering at BITS Pilani, started in AI-led product work at Blenheim Chalcot and Axion, then joined Vegapay's Founder's Office to launch lending and card programs with banks. Today I use AI tools like Claude Code to build the workflows I used to only write specs for.
