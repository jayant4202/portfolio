About me

I'm a product manager who turns ambiguous problem statements into structured, shippable solutions. I start with the problem and the people who have it, shape the solution with the teams who'll build it, and measure what ships. I use AI throughout: to build working prototypes and functional PRDs, so ideas are tested before they're expensive.

Most recently I was in Vegapay's Founder's Office, launching Credit Line on UPI and credit card programs with banks. Before that I built AI-led products at Axion and Blenheim Chalcot. I studied engineering at BITS Pilani.
