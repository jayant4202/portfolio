Volopay Invoice Rail: e-invoicing built through partners

Two questions from Volopay: which e-invoicing product should it launch through partners, and how should it build a partnership channel for its accounts-receivable product?

CompanyVolopay
MarketsIndia & UAE
WhenOctober 2026
Format2-page strategy + research

Where the opportunity is

India already mandates e-invoicing above ₹5 crore turnover, and the MSME payment rule pushes buyers to pay small suppliers on time. The UAE's first wave goes live on 1 January 2027, with 65+ accredited providers and buyers forced to choose one fast. Both markets suddenly have structured, government-validated invoice data.

The product: Volopay Invoice Rail

  1. Receive and send cleared e-invoices that auto-match to purchase orders, with no OCR.
  2. Pay approved invoices on the due date into supplier virtual accounts.
  3. Finance: once the buyer approves, the supplier can "get paid today" through a TReDS platform (India) or invoice lender (UAE). Volopay is never the lender.
  4. Reconcile payment, invoice and ERP posting automatically.
Build vs partner: Volopay builds the workflow, matching, eligibility and ERP layer; partners provide statutory clearance, licensed accounts and balance-sheet financing — each behind an adapter so it can be dual-sourced.

What the assignment covers

  • Partner shortlist with pricing, reviews and backups: Clear, RXIL, Cashfree, Wio Bank and Beehive.
  • Who to approach at each partner and why, with a 30-day plan and exit gates.
  • An AR partnership channel: 10 target partners, commercials, outreach messages and a 90-day activation plan with metrics.
