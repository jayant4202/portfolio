From "flying blind" to empowered agents: an AI support proposal

A solution-consulting case: a robotics company's new product launch flooded support, routine issues kept escalating to expensive engineers, and leaders had no early view of emerging problems.

CompanyAxionRay
Role appliedSolution consultant
WhenSeptember 2025
Format13-slide client deck

The situation

~3,000 US support calls a month across 200 agents, with 20% escalating to an engineering "SWAT" team at three times the cost. Knowledge was fragmented in a CRM that agents didn't trust.

The proposal

  • AI mines call transcripts in real time to spot emerging issue trends early.
  • It builds and updates a knowledge base of proven fixes automatically.
  • Suggestions appear inside the agents' existing CRM, with no new screen to learn.
  • Managers get live dashboards; engineering gets trend reports.

The business case

I modelled savings from fewer escalations across four scenarios. At a 25% reduction, payback is about 8 months; at 40–50%, about 4–5 months, against a $250k first-year cost. The deck also covers a four-phase rollout, a pilot-to-enterprise SaaS pricing model, each user group's role, and the change management needed for adoption.

Approach: lead with the customer's own words ("we're flying blind"), tie every feature to a cost they already feel, and de-risk the decision with a small pilot.
